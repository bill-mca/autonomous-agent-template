# Backlog

Outstanding jobs, one line each, most important first.
Ralph ticks take the first job that is not blocked.

Each job is a top-level list item: a bold name, any labels, a one-line description, and a link to its spec.
- `[ralph]` marks a job added by a Ralph tick.
- `[reflection]` marks a job added by a reflection.
- `[blocked]` marks a job waiting on the owner; see `BLOCKERS.md`.

When a job is finished, move its line to `progress.md`.
